# Care Behind the Quiet — redraw plan

Status: visual direction prepared; final assets not generated.

## Visual language

Observational conservation photography in Ya'an and the Sichuan panda habitat
system. The story is about skilled daily work, not visitor contact. Use humid
green forest, overcast daylight, worn practical surfaces, bamboo, tools, records
and respectful distance. Keepers and rangers should look focused and properly
equipped; the panda is calm and never posed for the camera.

Avoid cute advertising imagery, human-panda cuddling, costume encounters,
feeding by unqualified visitors, sterile fantasy laboratories, excessive close
contact, repeated eating portraits, and any scene implying that visitors carry
out professional animal care.

## Redraw continuity rules

- Keep staff uniforms, gloves and tools internally consistent without copying a
  real institution's marks or logo.
- The same adult panda may recur, but angle, behaviour and distance must change;
  never place two eating portraits next to one another.
- Human-animal distance must remain credible and ethically responsible.
- No visitor selfie, touching, hugging, costume or staged feeding scene.
- Finish with habitat rather than spectacle: the forest is the conservation
  subject, not merely a background.
