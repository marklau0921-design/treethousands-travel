# Story and activity image workflow

This is the standard production workflow for both website story pages and
activity pages.

## Required order

1. Confirm the story or activity theme, editorial direction, and written
   content.
2. Search for images that match each section of the confirmed content. Prefer a
   different source image for every placement and avoid visually similar images
   in adjacent sections.
3. Present the complete source-image selection to the client for confirmation.
4. Only after confirmation, regenerate each approved source image individually
   as a high-resolution, visually consistent image.
5. Review the complete regenerated set for quality, consistency, duplication,
   and page rhythm.
6. Only after the regenerated set passes review, place the final images into the
   website and update the page content.

Do not skip ahead. In particular, do not generate final images or wire images
into the website before the source-image selection has been confirmed.

## Regeneration meaning

Regeneration is a one-source-image-to-one-final-image process. Use the approved
source image directly as the visual basis and preserve its essential subject,
composition, viewpoint, action, environment relationship, and lighting
direction. Improve resolution, visual consistency, colour, and unsuitable
details as needed.

Do not combine several unrelated references to invent a new scene. Do not
replace the approved composition with a newly imagined shot.

## Reference and final-image rules

- All linked source images are `REFERENCE_ONLY` until approved and regenerated.
- A link is not evidence of website or commercial-use permission.
- Keep the source URL with every candidate so the selection remains traceable.
- Final images must contain no text, logo, watermark, or invented brand mark.
- The same final image must not appear in adjacent page sections. Prefer a
  unique image for every placement.
- Review saturation and clarity as a set. Keep a natural photographic result,
  but avoid an unnecessarily grey or washed-out grade.
- Story and activity pages follow this same workflow without exception.
