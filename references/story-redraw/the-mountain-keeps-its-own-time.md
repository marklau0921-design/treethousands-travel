## Visual language

Quiet editorial documentary photography in Western Sichuan. Late-autumn colour
is present but restrained: weathered gold, dark evergreen, stone grey, muted
earth and occasional first snow. Natural overcast light and changeable weather
are preferable to postcard-blue skies. Travellers remain small in the frame and
behave naturally. Villages, roads, fields and grazing land must feel inhabited
and functional rather than arranged as scenery.

Avoid hyper-saturated foliage, fantasy mountains, empty luxury-travel imagery,
fashion poses, implausible ethnic costume, oversized peaks, and repeating the
same valley or boardwalk composition.

## Redraw continuity rules

- Keep one coherent late-autumn weather system across the sequence, while
  allowing altitude to change the season.
- Use the same understated editorial grade across every output.
- Travellers may recur as a loosely consistent pair, but their faces should not
  be close or individually identifiable.
- Do not reuse the exact same clothing colour in consecutive human scenes.
- Keep roads, buildings, vegetation and altitude geographically plausible for
  Western Sichuan.
